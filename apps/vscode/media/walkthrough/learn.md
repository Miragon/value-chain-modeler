# Learn the basics

- **Steps** are the chevrons of the chain, **organizational units** the ellipses. Drag them from
  the palette at the top, or focus an entry and press Enter to place one in the middle.
- Select a step to append the next step, a sub-step or an organizational unit from its context
  pad, or to connect it to another step.
- Double-click an element (or press E) to rename it.
- **Keyboard shortcuts…** in the menu at the top left lists every key.
